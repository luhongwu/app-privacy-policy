## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "BoboBru" (the "App"), developed and published by BoboBru (the "Developer", "we", or "us"). The App is an AI hairstyle preview tool: you upload a local photo, select a hairstyle and hair color, and the App generates hairstyle preview images via a third-party AI API.

We value your privacy. This Policy explains what information is involved when you use the App, how that information is used and protected, and what rights you have. **Our design principle is to process only the minimum information necessary to deliver the core functionality of the App.**

Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using and uninstall the App.

**Scope**: This Policy applies only to our own data processing. Information processing by third-party services integrated into the App (such as the AI Provider, app stores, and services provided by Google) is governed by their respective privacy policies, and we are not responsible for those third parties' processing (see Section 7).

## 3. Photos You Provide and Their Use

### 3.1 Source of Photos

To generate hairstyle previews, you may provide a photo by:

- Using the device **camera** to take a photo;
- Selecting an existing photo from the device **photo library**.

### 3.2 Use of Photos

The photo you select is used only for the following purposes:

1. Local, on-device format validation and compression;
2. Upload over an **HTTPS encrypted connection** to the third-party AI image processing API to generate the hairstyle preview;
3. After your confirmation, the generated image is saved to your device photo library by your own action, or retained in the App's local history.

To secure the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot faults, and carry out necessary internal quality and compliance reviews, we may perform technical processing on photos (for example, anomalous-call detection, automated detection of unlawful content, and resolution/format validation). We do not use this to identify you.

### 3.3 What We Do Not Do

- Your photo is processed only for as long as is necessary to generate the hairstyle preview and is not otherwise retained by us;
- Your photo is **not** used by us for advertising, profiling, or training AI models (processing by the third-party AI Provider is described in Section 7);
- Except for the third-party AI processing API described above and the service providers listed in Section 7, your photo is **not** transmitted to any other party;
- Photos you upload may contain facial information, which is sensitive personal data; by uploading you give your **separate consent** to its use for generating hairstyle previews and for the related security and compliance processing. **You may withdraw that consent at any time via the contact channel in Section 13; after withdrawal you will no longer be able to use the photo-processing features, and quota already consumed and fees already paid will not be refunded by reason of the withdrawal.**

## 4. Data We Store

### 4.1 Data Stored Only Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and is **not uploaded to any server**:

| Data type | Storage | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| History records (hairstyle ID, color ID, remote links and local file paths of generated images, timestamps) | App private directory | Showing your generation history |
| Theme (dark/light, theme color) and language preference | App private directory | Remembering your interface preferences |
| In-progress generation task information | App private directory (expires after 24 hours) | Resuming unfinished generations after interruption |

**Note on "service usage statistics"**: The App itself **does not maintain a separate local "usage statistics" database**. The anonymized events collected by Firebase Analytics (Section 7) are the App's only source of application-level usage statistics. Locally we retain only a "last generation timestamp" for task recovery (see "in-progress generation task information" above), and no other statistics fields.

You can delete all of the above local data at any time by uninstalling the App; history records can also be deleted manually within the App.

### 4.2 Data Stored on Servers

To verify subscription entitlements, manage AI generation quota, send generation-completion notifications, and prevent abuse, we store the following records on the Developer's own servers (Firebase Firestore, provided by Google Cloud):

| Data | Description | Purpose |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Random device identifier (UUID) | As described in Section 2, generated on your device by the App | Request-signature verification, quota counting, anomalous-call detection |
| Free-trial usage record | Number of free trials already used by that device | Administering the free trial allowance (see Section 2.5 of the Terms of Service) |
| Subscription credential identifier and entitlement status | Purchase credential, subscription product, validity period, and status returned by the app store | Determining whether a subscription is valid; determining per-period quota and reset cycle |
| Device list linked to that credential | Device identifiers and timestamps that have used the same purchase credential | Supporting use of one entitlement across multiple devices on the same app store account; detecting account sharing and bulk abuse |
| Generation quota usage count | Generations used in the current billing period | Rate-limiting in accordance with plan quota |
| Push token (FCM token) | Issued by Google | Sending you a system notification when an asynchronous generation completes (requires your notification permission) |
| Security and anti-abuse records | Integrity-verification failure counts, call-behaviour characteristics, and similar metrics | Detecting emulators, scripts, or automated bulk calls |
| Service and security logs | Request time, endpoint path, response status, and error codes | Maintaining service stability and security, troubleshooting, and handling billing and entitlement disputes |

These records **do not include** your name, contact details, precise location, or photos you upload. We retain them for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes above, maintain service security and stability, handle disputes, and comply with legal obligations (retention periods are set out in Section 4.4). Other than the cloud services hosting this data and the service providers listed in Section 7, we do not sell or rent these records to any third party; to fulfil the purposes in this Policy or comply with legal obligations, we may share necessary information within the scope described in Section 2. Deletion is described in Section 9.

**Anonymized and aggregated data**: Data that has been irreversibly anonymized or de-identified such that no individual can be identified (for example, aggregate call volumes, feature usage rates, and success rates) is not personal information, and we may use it for service operations, statistical analysis, security risk control, and product improvement without being subject to the purposes and retention limits stated in this Policy.

## 5. Device Permissions

The App requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality:

| Platform | Permission | Purpose |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Android | INTERNET | Calling the AI API to generate hairstyles and downloading generated images |
| Android | CAMERA | Taking a photo for hairstyle generation (triggered only when you tap to take a photo) |

Note: The App **no longer requests** `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`; generated images are saved through the system media-library APIs (MediaStore / PhotoKit), which require no additional storage permission. All permissions are triggered only when you actively use the corresponding feature, and the App does not invoke them in the background. **You may refuse or revoke these permissions at any time; after refusal or revocation the corresponding features may be unavailable, but this does not affect other features and does not constitute a breach by us of any obligation to provide those features.**
