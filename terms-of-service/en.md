## 2. Service Description

### 2.1 Service Content

The App is an **AI hairstyle preview tool**. You upload your photo, select a hairstyle and hair color, and the App generates hairstyle preview images via a third-party AI image processing API. Key features include:

- Taking a photo or selecting one from your photo library;
- 60+ hairstyles (male/female categories) and 22 hair colors to choose from;
- AI asynchronous generation of hairstyle preview images;
- Saving generated results to the system photo library and managing history records;
